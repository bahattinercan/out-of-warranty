### 1.3 Generated artwork and procedural assets

- `assets/robots/`, `assets/weapons/`, `assets/parts/`, `assets/portraits/`,
  `assets/bg/`, `assets/tex/`, `assets/splashes/` — produced with generative
  image models and then processed by `tools/art-prep.py` (palette/keying/crop/
  WebP). Model provenance and the commercial-use audit live in
  `docs/plans/art/ai-visual-production-plan.md` §8 and `docs/plans/art/art-production-plan-ai3090.md` §11.
- `assets/robot-models/` — generated by this repository's own tooling
  (`tools/robot-models.js`), no third-party rights involved.
- Everything else on screen (robot rig, terrain, sky, projectiles, skill
  effects, UI) is drawn procedurally in `js/robots.js`, `js/maps.js`,
  `js/terrain.js`, `js/skill-fx.js` and friends.
- All audio is synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API (`js/audio.js`).
  No sampled music, no third-party sound library.

Attribution in the UI: Settings → *Credits & License* tab.

## 3. What is *not* used

No web framework, no bundler, no npm runtime dependencies, no external web
fonts at runtime (fonts are local), no analytics or telemetry service, no
third-party audio or image files beyond the generated art above.
